# Phase 30 cell library - E1 family, eps_r 11.2, 10.05 GHz (MODEL_ONLY, NOT_HFSS_VALIDATED beyond the E1 eigenmode points)

Cell: RO3010 0.635 mm (eps_r 11.2 = datasheet design Dk, tan d 0.0022), 3.2 mm lattice, full-fill super-ellipse n = 4 (3.05 mm, gap 0.15 mm), centre slot L x W, outline fixed, slot turns. Mesh 48 (HFSS agreed with m48 to <1 % in f on E1: `HFSS_EIGEN_E1.md`).
Slot sizes are even multiples of the mesh cell (0.0667 mm): L = 0, 0.80, 1.20, 1.47, 1.73, 2.00, 2.27 mm; W = 0.13, 0.27, 0.40, 0.53, 0.67 mm (31 slots; odd cell counts collapse onto the even one below, so they were not used).
Scripts: `scripts/research/phase30_library.py` (uniform | lut | heldout), job `jobs/phase30-library.md`. Data: `results/phase30/library/`.

## Angle-dependent LUT (`angle_lut_v1.npz/json`)
Y[slot, a, psi, 2, 2]: full Bloch sheet admittance of the cell with the slot turned by a = 0..165 deg (15 deg steps) at kt = 1.30 k0 along psi = 0..165 deg; 31 x 12 x 12 = 4464 MoM tensors (8340 s on 40 cores).
Checks: exact C4 of the square lattice (relative error 1e-13 max), reciprocity Y_xy = Y_yx. Interpolation: trigonometric (period 180 deg) in a and psi, monotone PCHIP in L through L = 0, linear in W.
Held-out validation (`heldout.json`): 60 random points with slot lengths NOT in the table (0.93, 1.07, 1.33, 1.60, 1.87, 2.13 mm), random W, random a and psi in [0, 180): relative error of Y median 0.21 %, p90 0.81 %, max 3.0 % (worst: L 2.13, W 0.40 mm, near the steep part of M(L)); error in delta = (Y_xx - Y_yy)/2 median 0.013 mS, max 0.125 mS (delta ~1-3 mS). **No azimuth error above 5 %: the diagonal sweep is not triggered.**
Caveats: kt fixed at 1.30 k0 (beta of the slots varies 1.15-1.29); the LUT validates the INTERPOLATION against the same MoM, not the MoM against HFSS; only E1 at three points has an HFSS comparison, and the HFSS diagonal (53/53 deg) was +18 % away from the model (unresolved).

## S1 supercell: Q 403 vs model 45-91 (HFSS f 10.068 GHz matches the model 10.07)
1. Geometry: S1 STEP = 7 cells of E1 (1.89 x 0.58), 22.4 mm supercell, slot angle 0, 25.7 ... 154.3 deg, outline fixed, M constant (rotation only), gap 0.15 mm (step_check.json). It is the intended rotating-axis supercell; the HFSS run used ph_x 6.74 deg (correct for eps_r 10.2, 0.4 deg for 11.2; effect on f ~0.04 GHz).
2. Floquet for exactly that S1 (eps_r 11.2, f 10.068 GHz, N = 7, 25.7 deg/cell, `s1_floquet_check.log`): alpha/k0 = 0.0373 (first order 0.0665, ratio 0.56), beta_F/k0 1.3015; insensitive to the harmonic truncation (n_max 3-8: 0.03722-0.03732). With v_g 0.160 c: Q = 1/(2 (alpha/k0)(v_g/c)) = 84 (Floquet), 47 (first order). HFSS Q 403 -> alpha/k0 = 0.0078, 4.8x (Floquet) to 8.5x (first order) lower. Scaling the modulation depth delta by 0.5/0.75 gives alpha x 0.40/0.76: the HFSS value needs delta ~0.3 of the model.
3. Likeliest reasons the MoM alpha is high: (a) LOCALITY: Y of each cell is the periodic-neighbour value; with 25.7 deg per cell and 0.15 mm gaps the neighbours differ strongly, so the true modulation of the current is not the staircase of uniform-cell tensors (untested here; a direct MoM supercell solve is the test); (b) M_t 0.70 - the model sits at the edge of its first-order/Floquet validity and alpha ~ delta^1.3-2 amplifies a ~25 % delta uncertainty (HFSS-checked Y is within 1.5 %, delta is a difference) plus the mesh-48 staircase spread (alpha 1.5-2x); (c) the HFSS side: Q = Re f / (2 Im f) of a standing-wave pair at the zone centre is converted with the TRAVELLING-wave v_g (model 0.160 c, HFSS value pending from the sweep), the Radiation boundary on the 3.2 mm-wide column reflects, and all 10 modes lie at Q 300-720 (~ 1/tan d = 455), i.e. the modes may be loss-limited. Discriminators: S1 with tan d = 0 (Q of the radiating mode must rise if loss-limited), S1 with a PEC lid (loss-only Q), S1 with PML.
Ruled out here: kt-dependence of the tensor (E1, 10.068 GHz, eps_r 11.2): Y_xx 16.50 / Y_yy 20.38 mS at kt = 0.02 k0 -> delta = (Y_yy - Y_xx)/2 = 1.94 mS; at kt = 1.3 k0 (Y_xx along x 16.84, Y_yy along y 21.06 mS) delta = 2.11 mS: +9 %, not a factor 5.
