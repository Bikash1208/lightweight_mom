## 7. Recommendation for Phase 13's forward model

Local periodicity, at this grounded 8 mm design point, holds within the stated tolerance only for small neighbour phase steps and degrades sharply approaching resonance (Sec. 3); a reflectarray-style lookup-table forward model is therefore defensible ONLY if the aperture's own phase gradient per cell is kept at or below the measured max-step-within-tolerance everywhere (a real, checkable design constraint, not an assumption), and the periodic phase-gradient supercell (Sec. 3) shows this constraint is hard to satisfy near resonance specifically -- an aperture whose gradient must pass through that region cannot rely on a pure lookup table there. A full-aperture integral-equation solver (a la Budhu & Grbic 2021) remains the more broadly defensible choice unless the design is constrained to stay in the small-step-safe region measured here; this is offered as a recommendation, not a decision.
