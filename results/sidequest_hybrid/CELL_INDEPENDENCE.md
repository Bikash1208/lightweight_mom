# Cell independence: why the MoM missed the HFSS single-diode result, a redesign, and the scalar fallback (2026-11-05) — MODEL_ONLY, everything coded stays on hold
## HFSS (x run, 90 deg; air box ground-to-lid, pec metal, clean solves; DX/DY = R 2.44 ohm or C 28.16 fF)
C/C **10.670**, R/C **10.665**, C/R **10.690**, R/R **11.006** GHz: the cell switches only with both diodes ON (+0.336 GHz); each diode alone does not (the "DX ON 11.006" of the previous message was R/R). The model's additivity (crossed effect < 0.003 GHz, dY -4.2 mS per axis) is falsified. The earlier calibration from "two points" (OFF loading weaker, alpha 0.84) was based on the wrong reading; what stands is the pair C/C -> R/R (below). The held-calibrated board runs started on that reading were discarded.

## 1. Why the MoM predicts a single diode to switch (exact exported geometry, `sidequest_cell_independence.py`, Y_xx at f = 10.8 GHz, kt = 245.4 rad/m)
* **The MoM bridges only the footprint (1.0 mm of the slit), not the whole slit** - verified on the geometry mask and by a strap-length scan: dY_xx for an ideal metal strap in the top half of the vertical slit: 0.5 mm -3.66, 1.0 mm -3.81, 1.5 -3.89, 2.0 -3.92, 2.5 mm (the whole half) -3.93; both halves -5.0; the entire slit -5.15 mS. The effect saturates at once: it is **current crowding (strap + spreading inductance, 1/(omega L) ~ 3.8 mS, L ~ 4 nH) in parallel with the slit capacitance**, not the bridged length.
* **Not a mesh artefact:** strap 0.4..1.2 mm, dY_xx = -4.14 / -3.77 / -3.57 mS at mesh 32 / 64 / 96, drifting slowly (the slit/rim features converge from above), no collapse. Diode loads vs ideal strap: ON -3.87 vs SHORT -3.81 mS; OFF (28 fF) +2.23 mS vs open.
* **Cross coupling in the MoM:** y strap alone changes Y_xx by +0.07 mS (x diode alone changes Y_yy by 0.02): independent. With both: Y_xy 0.56 mS.
* **The "missing rims at the top and right ends" hypothesis** (user's view of the HFSS model): MoM with the rim removed at the top of the vertical slit and the right of the horizontal slit (diodes as exported): OFF/OFF 11.32-11.39 GHz (HFSS 10.670), x diode alone still switches (ON/OFF 10.995). It reproduces neither HFSS number: **rejected** (the symmetric-rim model matches HFSS C/C and R/R).
* **Where the MoM agrees with HFSS:** C/C: HFSS 10.670, MoM with OFF = open slit 10.662 (OFF = 28 fF load: 10.525); R/R: HFSS 11.006, MoM 11.014 (implied Y 10.07 -> 6.94 mS; MoM 10.14 -> 6.86). **Where it does not:** R/C (MoM 10.99 vs 10.665) and C/R (10.53 vs 10.690) - and the 28 fF diode capacitor, which has no effect in HFSS (C/C = open).
* **Diagnosis:** the half-bridging explanation is not supported by the MoM (a half-bridged slit already gives ~75 % of the full effect). Candidates on the HFSS side to test (`HFSS_SETUP.md`): the TM0 band tracked per state (a second band near 10.67 GHz would give a state-independent number), the RLC boundary values/current lines of single-diode states (R = 0.01 ohm; DY removed; C = 282 fF), mode identification. Until resolved, **independent switching is not confirmed in HFSS, the both-diode scalar switch is**.

Per-axis ON-only states are the second and third columns of each row (y-run values follow by x<->y symmetry); both-halves bridging makes each axis independent in the MoM (cross coupling 0.01-0.12 mS), and C1 keeps the rim path as a parallel conductor. **Recommended for the next HFSS check: C1** (`hfss_exports/sidequest_rt5880_cell_4diode/`, 4 sheets `DX_TOP`, `DX_BOTTOM`, `DY_RIGHT`, `DY_LEFT`, all geometry checks PASS, all four states predicted). Cost: 4 diodes per cell and 2 control lines (x pair, y pair); the diode count of any board doubles per switched axis. Non-crossing slit layouts (offset / L-shaped) cannot fully cut an axis (the rim or another path always remains), so they were not pursued; alpha for C1 is the first-order own-mean value with OFF = open and is **not yet HFSS-confirmed**.

## 3. Fallback: the current cell as a scalar 1-bit switch (both diodes on one line)
HFSS-supported cell numbers: C/C 10.670 -> R/R 11.006 GHz: isotropic switch Y 10.07 -> 6.94 mS (dY -3.13 mS), **alpha_beam 0.844 Np/m**, beta/k0 1.073 (`hfss_alpha_backout.py`). A switched cell then has 2 diodes on ONE control line (1 line + inverter for the whole board); the radiating current is radial (J = m rho_hat E): **linear polarisation only**, so K = 2 means two beams of the same polarisation (phi 0 / 180, theta 25). Engine `sidequest_scalar_board.py` (static cells: analog library levels or idealised +-1; library shapes from normal-incidence MoM; AA0 from the MoM, HFSS Q pending). Best polarisation: phi-hat (LP-V); theta-hat (LP-H) is worse (SLL -9..-12 dB).
