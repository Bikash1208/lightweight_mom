**Mode: mode 1 (lowest, TM-like bound mode below the light line; light line at 90 deg 6.461 GHz).** Record f and Q; check Ez over the patch, decaying upward (a higher mode with Ez ~ 0 is TE-like: do not use).
**Run order = `priority` in `results_template.csv`** (1 first). Tier 1 = the phase where the model puts the mode at 5.8 GHz (rounded to 5 deg); tier 2 = +-5 deg (priority 5), to bracket 5.8 GHz if tier 1 is more than about 0.15 GHz off. **Priority 1: S1 bare substrate, then ACROSS y: variant 1 OFF (95 deg), ON (80 deg) and the ABSENT reference (90 deg);** priority 2: OFF and ON across y in variant 2 (vias) at the same phases; priority 3-4: ALONG x (85 deg) variants 1 and 2.
**What the model expects (MoM, no vias, mesh 64, mesh-snapped dimensions).** ACROSS y at the tier-1 phases (ABSENT 90 deg, OFF 95 deg, ON 80 deg, from linear interpolation of the 5.8 GHz branch): ABSENT 5.765 GHz, OFF 5.816 GHz, ON 5.702 GHz (the `f_MoM` of the runs are quadratic fits; same phase, different numbers: compare at equal phase in `results_template.csv`) (`predictions.json` `states`, `runs`). **If HFSS shows OFF = ON across y, the sheet or the RLC is not active.** **ON across y has a RESONANCE in the model (island + closed diode, pole near 5.65 GHz, beta/k0 up to 1.39 at 108 deg): at the same phase there are two modes, the lower one at about 5.2-5.6 GHz (island resonance, phases 80-109 deg) and the TM mode near the light line above 5.7 GHz (beta/k0 1.00-1.02); the 5.8 GHz branch is the UPPER one, so for the ON state HFSS mode 1 will probably be the LOWER mode: record modes 1-3 with Ez and compare with `f_MoM_noVia_GHz` (upper) and `f_MoM_other_branch_GHz` (lower).** The variant-2 predictions are the same numbers (no vias in the MoM): the difference HFSS(vias) - HFSS(no vias) at the same phase is the via/anti-pad/stub effect; report it per run.
**Known model-vs-HFSS differences.** MoM geometry is the mesh-64 mask (clearance 0.544, gap 0.906, island 4.350 x 1.450, slot 8.700 x 2.900) while HFSS uses nominal 0.600 / 1.000 / 4.348 x 1.300 / 8.696 x 2.900; the MoM lumped sheet is 1.00 mm long over a 0.906 mm gap. Frequencies can differ by more than the notch-cell package (0.1-0.3 GHz is possible); the OFF-ON ordering and sign across y are the robust check. FR-4 values are assumed.
