# Phase 20 R3 item 1 — literature and topology selection (LP and CP 2-bit PIN-diode cells)

**How this was checked (read before trusting a row).** No PDF is committed or was available to me. Titles, DOIs, years and abstracts were taken from
Crossref / OpenAlex (queried by DOI); the Sci. Rep. 2017 paper is open access and its full text was read through Europe PMC. Every other paper is paywalled
here: its row says **abstract only**, and any detail not in the abstract is marked **not read**. Values from search-engine summaries are marked **(search summary)**
and are *not* verified. Nothing below is a measurement or a simulation of this project.

## Result in one paragraph
There is **no published planar, single-layer, 2-bit PIN-diode cell with all diodes in the patch plane and no vias at X-band that I could verify** for either
polarisation. The X-band 2-bit LP cell (Ma 2022) and the 2-bit LP cell of Zhao 2025 put the phase shifter in a reflection-type circuit *behind the ground plane*
(outside this solver's model: single substrate layer, PEC ground). The in-plane gap-diode LP cells are at 4 GHz and 7.25 GHz and use vias. The CP cells are in X-band
(crossed bowtie) and Ku-band (Pancharatnam–Berry); their diode counts and via use are **not in the abstracts**. So every candidate below needs a scaling step (a design step of
this project, not a published result), and the targets of the brief (−1.5 dB, ±15°, ±20 % diode tolerance) are *not* shown to be reachable by any of them.

## What this means for the R3 targets (stated, not decided)
* The brief's targets are **not demonstrated by any of these**: the best published losses are < 1.0 dB (CP-2, Ku) and < 3 dB (C, X-band, circuit-based, not a candidate); the in-plane gap-diode LP cells report \|r\| > 0.85 (−1.4 dB) at 7.25 GHz.
  None reports a ±20 % diode-tolerance analysis, which R2 found essential: expect the tolerance row to be the hardest target.
* All candidates need **scaling to 10 GHz on 1.575 mm RT5880 / 10 mm period**; the diode (SMP1345, L_s 0.7 nH, C_T 0.19 pF) is not the diode the papers used (B: MA4SPS502 R_s 3.8 Ω, C_T 0.085 pF, L 0.65 nH), so the published phase curves do not transfer.
* **Open for you:** (1) accept these four or swap (e.g. replace LP-2 by the roadmap's own asymmetric two-gap cell without a published precedent); (2) the full texts of F, G, H (paywalled) would settle the planar-fit question and the diode counts — if you can place them locally (not committed), I will read them before item 2; (3) whether scaling a Ku-band PB cell to X-band is acceptable for CP-2, given diode parasitics do not scale.
