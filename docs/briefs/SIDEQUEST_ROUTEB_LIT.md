## 2. Ranking for adaptation to 10.5 GHz (retrievable evidence only; M1: dimensions are incomplete, so no design is started)
1. **#1 Zhao-Pan-Shen TAP 2026** - the only entry that is a PIN-switched surface-wave cell on a modulated metasurface with a defined bias path (one diode, cathodes common to ground, anode to a layer below the ground). It fails R10 (three laminates + bond) and was not reproducible earlier (our reproduction at 6.9 GHz: OFF not mesh-converged at 0.15 / 0.12 / 0.10 mm, OFF 544 / 214 / 440 ohm, ON 52-58 ohm against 223 / 101 ohm).
   **Missing to reproduce it (do not guess):** exact leaf geometry (lens width, chord, how r1 / r2 define the two arcs, tangency), inter-cell clearance, pad shape (length l 0.6 against width), diode footprint pad and gap definition (g 0.5), position of the bias line / via pad (w 0.18, phi1 0.3, phi2 0.2) and the leaf orientation relative to the diode axis, stack of the bias layers (given in the summary) and the via structure through the ground, the lid distance and the CST phase definition.
2. **#2 Wu / Honari TAP 2025** - principle: the switching circuit sits BELOW the ground and is connected to the radiating metal by a via, so the surface metal carries no bias network and the contrast is obtained with an impedance-matched wideband structure (not a resonance). Adaptable to a grounded surface-wave cell only as a concept (back-side loaded stub switched by one diode); the cell is a reflect cell, not a surface-wave cell.
   **Missing:** dipole and slot dimensions, substrate stack and heights, the reflecting-circuit topology below the ground, via diameter, diode package pads, the impedance conditions they derive (equations).
3. **#3 Zhang et al. IEEE Access 2019** - X-band, small cell (0.365 lambda), PIN diode and bias on the rear side (the surface side stays clean), measured 19.2 dBi; the same back-side concept as #2 at the right band.
   **Missing:** operating frequency, diode count per cell and type, patch and slot dimensions, stack, via structure, bias network.
Not ranked: #5 (R2: 4 diodes; kept for R11 / R7 reference), #4 (diode model method: use for M4, not as a cell), #6 and #10 (radiating or 1-D), #7-#9 (varactor, resonant HIS, waveguide-fed).

## 3. Consequences for the design phase
* No published switchable surface-wave cell supplies beta and alpha in both states over x / y / 45 deg: item 2 must produce them (Bloch eigen solves at 90-120 deg, three meshes, own HFSS check).
* The two practical architectures in the retrievable record are (a) a surface cell with one diode bridging two metal halves and the bias carried through vias to layers below the ground (#1), and (b) a back-side switched circuit under the ground connected by a via (#2, #3). (b) removes the bias network from the surface, which is what R3 / R6 / R7 need; whether a grounded TM surface-wave cell can be made with it is a design question for item 2.
