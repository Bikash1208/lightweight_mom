# Phase 20 R3b — CP-2 cell re-optimisation for 10.5 GHz (CLOSED 2026-10-23: not pursued, baseline CP-2 retained)

> **Outcome:** the optimisation was stopped after 249 evaluations and not resumed (user decision); the baseline CP-2 cell is retained, the R4 library stays valid. See `results/phase20_r3b/OUTCOME.md`. The plan below is kept as written for the record.


Branch `phase20-ris`. **Status: plan for the user's approval. No optimisation has been run.** Labels: MODEL_ONLY with an approved `HFSS_CALIBRATED` delta correction for the baseline cell only, PROVISIONAL_DATASHEET, VIA_OMITTED_APPROXIMATION.

## 1. Why (the first HFSS check of the RIS cell, 2026-10-23)
`docs/validation/README.md` §8 / `results/phase20_r4/HFSS_CHECK.md`: the PB ladder works in HFSS (four states, ~90 deg steps, 0.6 dB loss at 10.5 GHz), but
* the best point is **10.5 GHz** (|r_RR| -0.55 … -0.78 dB, opposite-handed -16.8 dB at theta 0, -11.9 dB at theta 30); at 10.0 GHz the HFSS cell is much worse than the F = 2n library (opposite-handed -8.4 vs -15.2 dB, |r_RR| -1.5 vs -1.2 dB);
* the steps are **not equal**: HFSS 98 / 92 / 102 / 68 deg (theta 0), 97 / 92 / 100 / 71 deg (theta 30) at 10.5 GHz; the short step is 4 -> 1 (stub 135 -> 0 deg). The MoM shows the same pattern, weaker (91 / 101 / 90 / 77): the axis stubs (0, 90 deg) and the diagonal stubs (45, 135 deg) are not equivalent on the square lattice. Step error 22 deg (HFSS) vs target 20;
* the **theta-30 opposite-handed level** (-11.9 dB HFSS) misses -15 dB;
* a rigid frequency shift does **not** explain the differences (fit: -0.05 GHz from |r_RR|, -0.1 … -0.15 GHz from the opposite-handed curve); the HFSS pass band is narrower than the library's.

Decision (user, 2026-10-23): **the RIS operating frequency is 10.5 GHz** (was 10 GHz); R5 designs at 10.5 GHz. Keep the 15 mm cell.

## 2. Goals (same R3 targets, now at 10.5 GHz, HFSS-calibrated where data exist)
Lattice deviation <= 15 deg, step error <= 20 deg (target for R3b: all four steps within 90 +- 10 deg), min |r_RR| >= -1.5 dB, spread <= 1 dB, opposite-handed <= -15 dB at theta 0 and 30 deg; tolerance corners C_T +-20 %, L_s 0.1 nH (assumed) at 10.5 GHz. Reported over 10.25-10.75 GHz.
Primary levers: (a) **separate axis and diagonal stub parameters** (length and width) to equalise the four steps; (b) circumradius; (c) the theta-30 opposite-handed term (HFSS: state 1 is the worst at -11.9 dB, the others are -16 … -25 dB; the library has state 4 worst at -10.5 dB, so the two disagree on which stub is responsible). Not touched: period 15 mm, substrate, diode values (MADP-000907-14020 PROVISIONAL_DATASHEET, L_s 0 assumed), diode span / pad.

## 4. Grating / quantisation limits of the 15 mm pitch at 10.5 GHz (`results/phase20_r3b/grating_limits.json`, `scripts/research/phase20_r3b_grating.py`)
lambda = 28.55 mm, P / lambda = 0.525.
* **No Floquet grating lobe for 60 deg steering from normal incidence at 10.5 GHz**: the principal-plane limit is 64.6 deg; 60 deg is allowed up to **10.71 GHz** (10.54 GHz with the 3 % Wood guard used by the library), so a 60 deg beam has essentially **no frequency margin above 10.5 GHz** (limit 59.2 deg at 10.75 GHz, 54.8 deg at 11 GHz).
* Largest plane-wave incidence with no higher Floquet order at 10.5 GHz (guard 3 %): 60.8 deg in the principal planes (phi 0 / 90), 69 deg at phi 15 / 75, > 89 deg on the diagonal planes. At 11 GHz the principal-plane limit drops to the 50s of degrees; the library registry already refuses those points.
* **Quantisation, not grating, is the issue**: 60 deg needs a phase step of 163.8 deg per cell (2.2 cells per period) - a 2-bit code can only follow it with long irregular sequences. Array factor of a 64-cell row (isotropic elements, no coupling), ideal gradient quantised to 2 bits: main lobe -0.9 dB; strongest other lobe re main -9.2 dB at 60 deg (-44 deg direction), -9.0 dB at 45 deg, -8.3 dB at 30 deg, -9.5 dB at 20 deg; 1 bit: the mirror lobe is equal to the main lobe (a 1-bit row cannot steer to one side). Continuous phase would give -13 dB near the grazing skirt at 60 deg (finite-array tail of the grating lobe at sin theta = -1.04). Quantisation-lobe suppression is an R5 topic (random-offset and phase-dithering codings); the 15 mm pitch itself does not forbid 60 deg at 10.5 GHz but leaves no band margin.

## 5. Cost estimate (measured, not a promise)
One candidate evaluation = 4 (theta, phi) points x ~14 s (mesh 60, F = 120, 20 load sets in one call) ~ 1 min; a Nelder-Mead run of ~300 evaluations ~ 5 h per start, three starts in parallel (memory 0.8 GB each) ~ 5-6 h wall; mesh-90/120 finalist checks ~ 1-2 h; HFSS package generation at F = 2n ~ 1.5 h (R3 package measured: ~2 h under load); library rebuild (R3b-4) ~ 6-8 h. Total before the second HFSS run: about 10-12 h. Stop-and-ask rule: if a stage would exceed 24 h or the grid/plan must change, I stop and ask first.

## 6. Risks and open questions for the user
1. **Tolerance at 10.5 GHz.** The MoM (library, nominal good at 10.5: step error 14 deg, opposite-handed -18.6 dB at theta 0) breaks under every diode corner at 10.5 GHz (C_T -20 %: step error 28 deg, spread 3 dB; L_s 0.1 nH: step error 36 deg) - a sharp resonance. The R3 objective includes these corners and may be infeasible at 10.5 GHz with this topology; fallback options: relax the corner set (e.g. +-10 % C_T), accept a lower-Q cell with larger amplitude loss, or centre the design at 10.25-10.4 GHz where the corners are milder. **Which do you prefer if the corners cannot be met?** HFSS has not measured the corners; R3b-3 asks for C_T +-20 % at 10.5 GHz.
2. **Transfer assumption.** The delta table is measured for the baseline geometry at phi = 0, 0.5 GHz sampling; applying it to a modified geometry is an extrapolation of the calibration. The second HFSS run is the check; until then results are labelled `HFSS_CALIBRATED_TRANSFERRED`, not HFSS-validated.
3. **HFSS modelling details to confirm (yours):** the Floquet-port de-embedding stopped ~1.24 mm short of the patch plane (fitted and removed; please check the port height / de-embed distance); diodes are parallel-RLC sheets with a negligible partner element (MoM: series RLC across the gap).
4. **Pitch vs stub length** remains open (axis stubs sit on the edge limit); R3b leaves the pitch fixed as you asked, and uses the diagonal stubs as the free length.
5. theta-30 opposite-handed -15 dB may not be reachable together with the ladder targets; if not, it is reported as a miss (never tuned away) and R5 treats it as beam contamination.
