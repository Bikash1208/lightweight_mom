## 2. What the literature says that bears on the RO3006 board
1. **The only journal items with a quantified recipe are #1 and #4**: (a) the modulation depth must rise with radius (non-uniform) so that the leaky wave is consumed before the rim (conversion about 95 % makes rim diffraction negligible); (b) low depth gives rim diffraction and sidelobes; (c) conversion above 80 % needs a feed with minimal direct radiation; (d) feed power should not go into azimuth sectors with little modulation (#4).
2. Nothing retrievable gives a journal-backed number for choke rings, corrugated edges, via fences or shorted-ring monopoles in a modulated-metasurface context: I searched for them and found only a patent on reactive edge treatment and conference papers (excluded). Those items are therefore **not** claimed.
3. For **coded / 1-bit holograms** the journals found (#8, #9, #10, #11) report that optimisation (software) lowers the sidelobes, but none gives the mechanism of a state-common static-cell cone; #10 uses time modulation (extra control).
4. Full texts were not read for #2-#19: the table must be completed from the papers before any of it is quoted in a report.
