- P=12.0 mm: aligned mesh 60 cells; design-frequency coverage 296.6 deg (largest gap 63.4 deg); resonant L~6.8 mm; grating onset (normal) 25.0 GHz, 45deg oblique 14.6 GHz (inside the 10-15 GHz band); surface-wave margin 1.00 x k0 at 10.0 GHz -> 0.18 x k0 at 15.0 GHz

Report measured coverage honestly (brief's own instruction): none of the three periods reaches a
full 360 deg with a single-layer resonant patch. As period grows, the achievable design-frequency
coverage and the surface-wave-pole margin both change — **measured, not assumed**; see
`period_study.json` for the full per-period, per-frequency tables and
`plots/coverage_vs_frequency_per_period.png`. Standard remedies for reaching fuller coverage
(thinner substrate, stacked patches, multi-resonant elements) are open options, **not implemented
here**.

## 6. Rules followed

No period or geometry was chosen by phase coverage alone; grating-order onset (normal and four
oblique feed angles), surface-wave-pole margin, loss (|S11|) and the measured coverage are all
reported side by side, and the choice is left to the user. No earlier phase's results were
modified in place; every new sweep here is written under `results/phase12b_mesh_period_study/`,
tagged with its own period and mesh, and registered as a benchmark only, never production data.
