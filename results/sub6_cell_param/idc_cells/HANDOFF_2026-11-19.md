## 5. Lessons
- The ON state must contain **only closed slots**; an open rim-to-rim cut across the closed slot kills the anchor-type anisotropy.
- OFF must be isotropic **by symmetry** (single-slot cells cannot switch: the diode only shifts a resonance).
- A diode needs two DC-separate nodes with bias paths; matched parasitics on all four arms.
- Shorted vias make a mushroom surface; bias vias must be RF-isolated and in the model.
- Resonance should sit about 0.15-0.3 GHz **above** f_op; resonance below band leaves the wave unloaded (B0p78, C4).
- Fine finger geometry becomes multi-moded and its mode assignment ambiguous (B1p5, D3p6).
- Hologram note: the cell's resonant axis sets the rotation offset; a uniform 90 deg offset adds a constant 180 deg CP phase and must be applied consistently.

## 76. User question: rectangular board instead of circular (advisor answer, REASONING not a result)
